# Blog archive

The machine-drafted versions of the first six posts, exactly as they were published before the blog
was reset in October 2026. Each post now lives in `content/blog/` as an outline that the author
rewrites in their own words in `/editorial`.

These files are reference, not content:

- Nothing reads this folder. The blog, the RSS feed, the sitemap, the social cards, and `/editorial`
  all read `content/blog/` only, so an archived post can never go live or appear in writing mode.
- The editor deliberately does not show them, so the rewrite is not anchored to the old wording.
  Open one yourself, after writing, when you want to compare.
- Agents may consult them for facts and verified links — `blog-refine` checks a draft's product
  claims against them alongside the real sources — but never for wording, voice, or structure, and
  never copy text from them into a post.
- Their frontmatter is stale: the dates are the old schedule, and none of them was ever approved.
