# Video hooks

The opening line of a reel, short or TikTok: the first two seconds, said or on screen, that decide
whether anyone watches the rest. Collected here by angle, so a new video starts from a tested line
rather than a blank page.

Scripts and storyboards are in [Video scripts and storyboards](video-scripts.md); the copy that goes
out without video is in [Launch copy](launch-copy.md).

## How to use this

The plan is to find the angle that resonates and then make it many times over. So:

1. **Test angles, not single hooks.** Post one video per angle within a week, at the same time of day,
   on Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
2. **Judge after two or three days** on saves and shares per 1,000 views and on how many people watch
   to the end. Likes say little. Write the numbers into the table for that hook.
3. **Push the winner.** Make ten more on the winning angle (new songs, new hooks from its list, new
   openings) before testing anything else.
4. **End every video asking for a comment**, such as "Comment what feature I should build next".
   Comments help the video spread and give a free feature backlog.

## Rules for a hook

- **It has to be true of the app today.** A reel that promises something the app does not do gets
  "it doesn't do that" in the comments, which is the one reply that hurts.
- **The picture matches the words.** If the hook asks a question, the next shot starts answering it.
- **Name the app chordlist, lowercase**, or do not name it at all in the hook.
- **No competitor screenshots.** Show a generic cluttered chord page rather than one somebody can
  recognise; "no ads, no account" makes the point without naming anyone.
